2024’S Ultimate Guide to the Most Advanced Smart Speakers: Thoroughly Tested and Evaluated | ZDNet

Gone are the days when a speaker’s only function was to play music. While there is still an array of quality speakers whose primary purpose is premium sound production, the smart speaker market is stocked with models that can simultaneously boost your mood with booming bass and also be a key piece of your smart home setup , even syncing with other smart home devices, setting reminders, and making calls.

The best smart speakers integrate voice control, easy Wi-Fi connections, and make a nice addition to your space. In fact, our top choice, the Sonos Era 100 is a sleek bookshelf speaker that our testing proved to deliver fantastic clarity and easy voice control. Read on to see how smart speakers from Apple and Amazon stack up after putting them to the test.

Amazon Echo Show 8 tech specs: Compatibility: Alexa, Zigbee, Matter, and Thread | Dimensions: 7.9 x 5.5 x 4.2 inches

The Echo Show 8 is Amazon’s latest smart speaker and features extensive upgrades from its 2021 version. It includes a new design with a centered camera, an updated processor for faster display interactions, and a smart home hub feature to control all your smart home devices from one place.

When it comes to the speaker, the Echo Show 8 offers a broader and more immersive sound experience due to its custom-built spatial audio processing technology. The Echo Show 8 not only includes better sound all throughout but also minimizes background noise and senses the acoustics of a room to adjust the noise level.

You can pre-order the Echo Show 8 for $150, which will begin shipping on October 25. Keep in mind the adjustable Echo Show 8 stand with USB-C charging port is not included with the smart speaker and must be purchased at an additional cost.

Also coming this fall is the Echo Show 8 Photos Edition for $160, which includes a six-month PhotosPlus subscription that allows you to make your photos and videos the primary content on your display.

How did we choose these smart speakers?

When searching for the best smart speakers, I considered a number of factors.

  • Integrations: The best smart speakers incorporate voice control, whether it is through Apple’s Siri or Google Assistant.
  • Connectivity: The best speakers offer a wireless connection via Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.
  • Size: Not all speakers are easy for on-the-go listening. If you want something that is easily portable, consider a smart speaker of a smaller size that offers a wireless connection.
  • Budget: We know that not everyone shares the same budget, so we look for smart speakers that suit a number of different pricepoints.

What is a smart speaker and which brands make the most popular ones?

A smart speaker is a type of audio device that combines the functions of a speaker with a virtual assistant and voice control. The most popular brands that make smart speakers are Amazon, Google, Apple, and Sonos.

Amazon’s popular smart speaker line is the Amazon Echo, which comes in a variety of sizes and styles, including the Echo Dot, Echo Show, and Echo Studio . These all have Alexa. Meanwhile, Google offers the Google Nest line of speakers with Google Assistant, and Apple’s smart speaker range (HomePod) has Siri. Then there is Sonos, which offers premium sound in conjunction with support for popular voice assistants.

This guide tells you which of these smart speakers are worth buying, and what you’ll get for your money.

What can you do with a smart speaker?

A smart speaker works like a traditional speaker in that it plays audio content. But that is far from all it does. Smart speakers also feature integrations like Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant, and provide access to your favorite streaming apps. You can also use a smart speaker for voice control, phone calls, and even video messages, depending on the smart speaker you choose.

Which smart speaker has free music?

The best smart speakers offer a wireless connection that allows you to connect to a number of programs. Stream from services like Pandora, Apple Music, and SiriusXM, with the cost all depending on which subscription you have.

How much do the best smart speakers cost?

Smart speakers can range significantly in cost, as seen here. The best smart speakers range in cost from $50 to about $250, depending on the model you choose.
