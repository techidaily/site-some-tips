Leading Cloud Server Providers Vetted by Industry Professionals – A Complete Analysis on ZDNet

There are plenty of cloud hosting options to choose from. Selecting the right cloud hosting service for you or your business can be tricky (and sometimes overwhelming), especially with the variety of features and pricing plans available. The good news is that you don’t need to be an IT expert to get started.

Cloud hosting providers offer everything from simple setups for SMBs to full-scale infrastructure solutions for large enterprises. They often include features like automated backups, security tools, and scalability options as part of their standard offerings.

What is the best cloud hosting service right now?

After extensive hands-on testing and research, our top pick for the best cloud hosting service in 2024 is Amazon Web Services (AWS) . AWS offers a comprehensive range of services that cater to all businesses, regardless of size. It also has a global network of data centers, which translates to low latency and high availability for companies with a global presence, and a flexible pay-as-you-go pricing model.

Read on for the rest of our top picks for cloud hosting services, ranging from budget-friendly choices to powerful, enterprise-level solutions.

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Best cloud hosting service overall

Amazon Web Services (AWS) is our top pick for the best overall cloud hosting provider, and for good reason. AWS is the backbone for some of the world’s largest companies. It offers everything from basic computing power to advanced machine learning tools. AWS is versatile enough to do pretty much anything any business needs.

AWS isn’t just for large enterprises, though. It offers tools and services that meet the needs of both startups and SMBs, like storage solutions and the aforementioned computing power.

One of AWS’s major green flags is its global infrastructure. Its data centers are spread across the globe. This means that if you’re a global (or fledgling company with big aspirations), your applications are closer to your users, which translates to faster load times and a smoother user experience.

AWS also lets you easily scale resources up or down, without switching platforms. This scalability is coupled with a pay-as-you-go pricing model, so you only pay for the resources you actually use. This may not mean much to giant corporations, but is huge for small businesses looking to scale.

AWS can be a bit overwhelming though, especially if you’re new to cloud hosting. While the platform offers extensive documentation and support, there’s no denying that the learning curve is steep.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP)

Best cloud hosting platform for scalability

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is a top choice for businesses that need scalable cloud infrastructure. GCP is built to handle a wide range of workloads, so it’s perfect for companies that have had or expect growth.

One major argument for GCP’s scalability is its integration with powerful tools like Kubernetes Engine for container management and BigQuery for data analytics. These tools are built to scale and allow you to manage and analyze large amounts of data.

Whether you’re dealing with a sudden surge in traffic or you’re expanding your operations, GCP can scale up (or down) without sacrificing responsiveness, reliability, and budget.

GCP’s pricing model, with sustained use discounts, also supports scalability. This allows you to manage your business effectively as you grow instead of being tied to a specific tier level.

With all the good is a potential drawback: The platform’s complexity can be a barrier for those new to cloud hosting, and support levels depend on the plan selected. They have a great documentation portal , though.

A2 Hosting

Best cloud hosting service for performance

If speed is what you’re after, A2 Hosting is a solid choice. With its high-speed SSD storage and Turbo servers, A2 Hosting is built to deliver fast load times and reliable uptime, which is necessary if your website needs to handle heavy traffic or you just want everything to run smoothly.

The Turbo servers and SSD storage really do make a major difference in site speed (and if you’re a content-heavy website, site speed = user experience = green flag with Google ). They make sure your site loads quickly and runs smoothly.

A2 Hosting also offers free site migration, which is a nice bonus if you’re looking to switch from another provider without the hassle.

On the downside, A2 Hosting has fewer data centers compared to some larger providers, which might impact performance depending on where your visitors are located. Also, keep an eye on renewal rates, as they can be higher than the initial pricing.

DigitalOcean

Best budget cloud hosting service

DigitalOcean is our pick for the best budget cloud hosting service. I personally use DigitalOcean for my recently launched website in Japan and also for a site I run in the US. It’s more than met my expectations for performance and, most importantly, budget.
The platform’s pre-configured droplets allow users to deploy virtual private servers in just a few minutes, which is particularly helpful for those with limited technical knowledge.

Even still, DigitalOcean does have a reputation for being a little difficult to get up and running, but with the extensive developer support and documentation, I had no troubles. This will vary between individuals, of course, but you can always contact support.

One of the biggest advantages of DigitalOcean is its transparent pricing. There are no hidden fees or complicated pricing tiers – what you see is what you pay. This makes it an attractive option for those working with tight budgets who need to keep costs predictable.

Microsoft Azure is our go-to pick for enterprises, especially if you’re already using Microsoft products like Office 365 or Dynamics 365. Azure’s integration with these tools makes managing cloud resources smoother and keeps everything in sync with your existing workflows.

But if you’re looking for a cloud platform that’s reliable, secure, and deeply integrated with Microsoft’s ecosystem, Azure is hard to beat.

Factors to consider when choosing a cloud hosting service

Choosing the right cloud hosting service is a big decision to make. Consider these factors first, and then decide which provider suits your needs best.

  • Reliability and uptime: The reliability of your hosting provider isn’t just important; it’s necessary. Look for services with high uptime guarantees (99.9% or higher) to ensure your website or application remains accessible to users with minimal downtime. (Downtime = lost revenue!)
  • Pricing and scalability: Understand the service’s pricing structure, and be sure to include those pesky hidden costs. Consider whether the provider offers the ability to scale resources up or down as your needs change without incurring significant additional costs.
  • Security features: You need to protect your (and your users’) data, so make sure that the security features are top-shelf. Look for features like SSL certificates, firewalls, DDoS protection, and regular backups. Also, make sure the provider complies with relevant regulations, especially if you handle sensitive data, so that you don’t get sued into oblivion.
  • Customer support: Reliable customer support can be a lifesaver if you encounter issues. Check the availability of support (24/7 is ideal), the channels offered (live chat, phone, email), and the quality of the support based on user reviews.
  • Ease of use and management: A user-friendly interface and straightforward management tools can save you time and reduce the overall complexity of running your site or application–especially if you don’t have an in-house developer. Consider whether the provider offers a control panel or other management tools that make it easy to handle tasks like setting up domains, managing databases, and monitoring performance.
  • Additional tools and integrations: What other tools does the hosting provider offer that could benefit you? How about content management systems (CMS) like WordPress, e-commerce platforms, and/or developer tools? Get the most bang for your buck.

Cloud hosting FAQs

What is the difference between cloud hosting and traditional hosting?

Cloud hosting uses a network of virtual servers that draw resources from multiple physical servers. This setup allows for easy scaling since resources can be added or removed as needed. If one server fails, another takes over. This means better uptime and reliability.

Traditional hosting relies on a single server. In shared hosting, multiple websites share the same server resources, which can lead to slower performance. In dedicated hosting, your site has its own server. This gives you better performance but at a higher cost. Scaling usually requires upgrading to a different server or plan.

Can you get cloud hosting for free?

Yes, you can get cloud hosting for free, but there are limitations. Some providers, like AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure, offer free tiers with limited resources, which are generally used for testing, small projects, or learning. These free options usually come with restrictions on storage, computing power, and support. You shouldn’t use a free tier for a large or high-traffic site.

Many providers also offer free trial periods. These usually last 30 to 90 days, after which you’ll need to switch to a paid plan to continue using the service.

In short, while free cloud hosting does exist and can be useful for small-scale needs, you’ll likely need to eventually upgrade to a paid plan.

Who is the largest cloud hosting provider?

The largest cloud hosting providers are AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. These three companies dominate the cloud hosting market.

What is the best cloud hosting for small businesses?

For small businesses, DigitalOcean and A2 Hosting are excellent choices. DigitalOcean also offers affordable, easy-to-use cloud hosting with transparent pricing – perfect for smaller businesses (including startups) who want to get online quickly without going bankrupt.

If you’re a small business that needs a fast and dependable website to serve your customers, A2 Hosting is the way to go.

All options offer the essential features small businesses need without unnecessary complexity or cost.
